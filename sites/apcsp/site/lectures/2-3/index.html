Extracting Information from Data

Data are not conclusions. We look for patterns in records while stating data quality, source limits, and what the evidence cannot establish.

← Back to the Big Idea 2 handouts

Success criteria

  • Distinguish data, information, and metadata.
  • Describe a trend or correlation without treating it as causation.
  • Identify incomplete, invalid, inconsistent, biased, or multi-source data challenges.
  • Write an evidence-based claim with a clear boundary.

Three levels of language

LevelExampleIt does not automatically mean
dataA post has tags, views, and comments.“This kind of post is always better.”
informationA tag appears eight times in this sample.A prediction about every user or future result.
metadataField names, units, date format, missing-value rules.The original post itself.

Mingcheng Community scenario: minimum necessary data

To discuss content organization, a lesson may use synthetic category, tags, and views fields. It does not need names, accounts, messages, IP addresses, devices, or precise reading histories. The goal is to understand content distribution, not evaluate people.

Inconsistent data

Do Tech, tech, and Tech mean the same category? Standardize equivalent values without changing meaning.

Missing or invalid data

n/a and blanks are not zero. Flag, skip, or handle them explicitly rather than inventing a value.

Bias

Collecting more records from the same biased source does not necessarily remove the bias.

Correlation is not causation

If posts tagged python have higher average views in a sample, that is a possible correlation. It does not show that the tag caused views: promotion, timing, and audience are unrecorded variables.

Original practice

  1. Write one question that categories, tags, and views can answer, and one that they cannot.
  2. Write two pieces of metadata for a table: a unit and a missing-value rule.
  3. Rewrite “rainy-day posts have more views, so rain causes reading” as a careful claim.
  4. Why should data-source collection methods be recorded when combining sources?

Exit ticket

“In this sample, ________; the evidence is ________.” “This does not show ________ because ________.”
